Professional Animal Works (PAW)

PAW is designed to handle the demands of growing pet care businesses while maintaining the highest standards of data security and privacy.

For Businesses

Complete appointment management system with comprehensive pet and owner profiles. New revenue streams through PAW services.

For Pet Parents

Effortless booking, live-service requests, and real-time updates. Stay informed with instant notifications.

TreatTap

Live treat sessions that bring pet parents closer to their pets throughout the day. Request 5 or 10-minute personalized sessions where facility staff provides treats, attention, and playtime while parents watch in real-time.

PAW Camera (PAWC)

Purpose-built camera for TreatTap. Ultra-light, weather-ready, and facility-friendly. HD live video for TreatTap sessions.
