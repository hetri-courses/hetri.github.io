About Hetri

We're revolutionizing pet care through innovative technology that brings peace of mind to pet owners and operational excellence to facilities.

Our Founder

Hetri was born from an observation: while technology has transformed many industries, pet care facilities were still relying on outdated systems.

Our founder, inspired by a Cal Poly Pomona marketing professor's challenge to "be different," set out to create innovative solutions for real-world problems.

This philosophy led to the creation of TreatTap and the PAW management platform, combining hardware with software to create seamless experiences for both pet owners and facility operators.
