Overview

Hetri Inc licenses its software products on a subscription basis. This page summarizes current pricing and links to the legal documents that govern each license. Purchases are completed on each product's own site.

Professional Animal Works (PAW)

Facility management software for pet care businesses. Licensed per facility as a business (B2B) subscription.

  • $49.99 per month per customer, or $499.99 per year; multi-tenant, covering every facility you operate
  • Unlimited TreatTap sessions included; facilities set their own client pricing and keep 100%
  • Full ERP suite included

Full details and signup: paw.hetri.org/licensing

Grotaro

A real-time market data and analytics dashboard for individual users, licensed as a consumer (B2C) subscription.

  • 14-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase

Full details and signup: grotaro.com
